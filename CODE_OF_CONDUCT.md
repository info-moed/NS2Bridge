# Code of Conduct

NS2 Bridge is a place to make controllers work on Macs, and to learn from each other while doing it. Everyone is
welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to this.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Give and take criticism about the work, not the person.
- Credit others' work and findings.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Publishing someone's private information (including controller serial numbers or Bluetooth addresses from their
  logs) without permission.
- Sharing piracy, ROMs, firmware or other material that isn't yours to share.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project
elsewhere. Report problems privately to the maintainer through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/info-moed/NS2Bridge/security/advisories/new) (marked "conduct"), or
by contacting the maintainer on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and warn, suspend or ban participants, in proportion to the harm.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
